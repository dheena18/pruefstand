[T5.3, balancing] Down-sampling to 50 % positives fills at most 60 % of the negative budget with hard negatives first (round-robin over `hard_type`, seeded), then off-topic negatives, then any remaining hard negatives. A literal "hard negatives first" would leave no off-topic negatives at all (each question yields up to four hard negatives), and W3.8 wants both (source: W3.8)
