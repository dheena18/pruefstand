[T4.5, rules vs model] `compound_split`: a rule finds capitalised words of >= 10 letters ending in a head of `perturb/data/compound_heads.yaml` (own list, no `rag_demo` import), the model paraphrases them (a compound it keeps = `unchanged`, no error). `register`: Sie <-> du by rule from `data/register_verbs.yaml` (46 verbs, the finite verb in front of `Sie`/`du`, `fuer Sie`/`mit Ihnen`, `Ihr...`/`dein...`); everything the table cannot conjugate (`ob Sie ... haben`, unlisted verb, sentence-initial `Sie`/`Ihr` that may mean "they") goes to the model, a rule-only rewrite makes zero calls. `colloquial`: model only. Rules never guess a verb form.
