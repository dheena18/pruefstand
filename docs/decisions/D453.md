[T3.2] One app, two profiles: `RagConfig.profile` is `live` (tier 1, llama.cpp + TEI) or `gpu` (tier 2, Ollama native API). A `gpu` config is validated against the model catalogue `RAG_MODEL_CATALOGUE` (`infra/gpu/models.yaml`, copied into the image) at config load: `catalogue_id` must be `ollama/<tag>` (D221) and exist with the right role, `model` is the catalogue tag, `options` (`num_ctx`, `top_p`) equal the catalogue (Y3.2), `generation.temperature` and `generation.max_tokens` stay inside `option_bounds` (N2). `endpoint_env` URLs are read and checked at app start, never at config load, so `rag.gpu.yaml` loads in CPU CI
