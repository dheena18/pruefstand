[T4.3, "der Text" rule] `check_overlap` also returns `meta_reference` when the question talks about the text it was written from: an article or `im/laut/gemäß/vom/aus` followed by Text, Textauszug, Abschnitt, Auszug, Ausschnitt or Passage (`der Text`, `laut Auszug`, `im Abschnitt`); for an English question `the/this/that` + text, passage, excerpt, extract. `Textilarbeiter` or a plain `Abschnitt` without article are not hits.
