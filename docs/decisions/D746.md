[T4.5, review round 2] (1) `gender`: a noun whose singular equals its plural is rule-converted only when an explicit plural determiner or number stands directly before it (only adjectives, at most three, between; no comma, noun or preposition phrase); every other context goes to the model. (2) The participle style is used by rule only where its declension is certain: weak `-n` behind `die/diese/alle/keine/meine...` (also with adjectives) and in the dative, strong behind `viele/einige/zwei...`; behind `welche` or without determiner the style is not drawn. (3) CLI: a question is private (local model only) unless it comes from a `shared_public` line `tenant<TAB>question` or the operator passes `--public`; the flag applies to all three passes and `--no-canaries` does not change it. (4) Only `DataGuardViolation` becomes `no_independent_validator`; other `PolicyViolation`s surface. `register`: sentence-initial `Ihnen`, joint subjects (`Sie und ...`) and quoted speech go to the model. A truncated last line of `rewrites.jsonl` / `verdicts.jsonl` is skipped (and cut off on the next write); an invalid earlier line is a `DatasetError`; two items with one id but different text or privacy raise `DatasetError`.
