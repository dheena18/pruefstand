[T4.7a, smoke selection] `select_smoke(cases, items, seed, size=50, min_per_category=3, min_probes=5)` is the rule: whole clusters of unperturbed cases whose every case is a model-`ok` item without a deterministic finding, seeded; every category present gets at least 3 cases (else `DatasetError`), at least 5 hard-fail probes (leak, injection, system prompt, filter bypass) are included, the rest is filled to the size. The T4.1 `pick_smoke` still fills `smoke` at split time (before any review exists); wiring `select_smoke` into the re-selection after the review is a T4.7b step with the real build. |
