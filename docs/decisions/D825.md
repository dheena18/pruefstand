[T4.3, orchestration and outputs] `filter_candidates` (module `dataset.filters_run`) runs chunk presence, `check_evidence`, `check_overlap` per source chunk, `drop_cluster_overlap`, the support check and `dedupe`, in that order (support before dedupe so an unsupported candidate never shadows a good near-duplicate); a candidate is dropped at the first stage that rejects it, with all reasons of that stage. `scripts/filter_qa.py <run>/candidates.jsonl` writes `filtered.jsonl`, `filtered_out.jsonl` (every dropped candidate with its reasons) and `filter_report.json` (counts per reason, `not_checked`, `support: checked|skipped`, the style report, the config), prints `kept=<k> unsupported=<a> ngram_overlap=<b> near_duplicate=<c>`; exit 3 when a quota/cap/`judge_error` left candidates unjudged (re-run), 2 for refusals. `--no-support` is allowed and says so in the report.
