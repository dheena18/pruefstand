[T4.7a, owner spot-check and trust rule] `sample_spotcheck`: all `smoke` items first (their citations are always checked, even above the size), then a seeded draw of model-`ok` and model-`fix` items stratified by category and verdict up to 50. The app (`tools/review_app/`, Streamlit, not under `apps/`) shows question, answer and gold citation on the left and the source chunks of the gold document on the right with the cited Absatz highlighted (the evidence sentence stronger), asks two yes/no questions (`y`/`n` keys), takes a pseudonym in the sidebar and writes `spotcheck_decisions.jsonl` (`case_id, citation_ok, plausible, note, reviewer=owner-spotcheck:<pseudonym>, ts`); it never writes into `datasets/`, only `apply` does (which adds one `ReviewRound` per spot-checker with `prompt_version="spotcheck"`). `spotcheck_trust`: the model review is NOT trusted when more than 10 % of the spot-checked model-`ok` items are flagged (citation or plausibility "no"); 4 of 40 is trusted, 5 of 40 is not; flags on items the model already fixed do not count. |
