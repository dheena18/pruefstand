[T4.2, sampling] Only chunks of the public sources `gii`, `eurlex`, `oldp`, `germanquad`, `germanrag` that are not attack chunks and have at least 40 tokens are sampled (synthetic private documents and canaries are never sampled; whatever slips through still meets the `LLMClient` guard and is counted `skipped_private`). Per tenant, stratified round-robin over `source`, at most one chunk per document (one document = one cluster), seeded permutations. The documents in `indomain_dev_sources.json` `source_passage_ids` are excluded (D581, D602). Multi-hop pairs: neighbouring paragraphs of one law (same tenant) or a law paragraph and a decision that cites it in the citation graph; cross-lingual pairs: the chunks of one `parallel_id` aligned by their `#k` position (skipped when the DE and EN chunk counts differ); a pair never repeats a chunk; pairs never cross tenants; a pair that would mix two share-alike licences (`license_config_of` is None) is dropped before any request. Private-document, canary and held-out generation (local judge model, D330) is not part of this task.
