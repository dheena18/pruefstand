[T1.7, D349 follow-up; revised in review] **Unverifiable hard-fail checks.** "Target declares chunk support" = at least one recorded response of the baseline **or** the candidate has `chunks is not None`. For a case that was answered (no recorded response-less request and `error_rate != 1`; without records every case counts as answered): a hard-fail metric that is `error` is **always** a `HardFailFinding(rule="hard_fail_unverifiable")` (the answer-only leak checks can error on any target, e.g. regex budget); `not_applicable` with a reason other than `category` / `no_forbidden_strings` is one on a chunk-declaring target; a metric absent from the case is one. Non-binary hard-fail values raise `GateInputError`. Cases whose request failed belong to the `error_rate` check. Known limit, follow-up: `truncated=True` of a citation metric should at least warn (T1.8/T1.10).
