[T6.2a S4b, #137 low findings] `llm-proxy`: (1) a body nested too deep for the parser (`RecursionError`, e.g. 100 000 levels) is a 403, not a dropped connection; (2) the two reserved `/health` slots get a 2 s total deadline (`reserve_deadline_s`; normal slots keep 60 s), so tricklers on them cannot starve `/health`; (3) a body shorter than `Content-Length` (cut or half-closed) is a 403 "body shorter than Content-Length" and never forwarded; (4) `log_message` reads `path`/`command` only when set, so the stdlib 400/414/505 for a malformed request line is actually sent; `seed` must be an integer in [0, 2^31 - 1] (int32), else 403 "option out of bounds: seed"
