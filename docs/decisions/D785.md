[T4.2, INTERFACE CHANGE, additive, flagged in the PR] interfaces §14: `QACandidate` gains `evidence_span` (pre-approved, round 9), `extra_chunk_ids` (the second chunk of a pair; `chunk_id` stays the gold chunk), `lang` (language of the question) and `style` (`laie`/`fachlich`); `generate_qa` and `generate_qa_pairs` (pre-approved) gain the keyword-only options `concurrency`, `stats` (`GenStats`), `sink` and `skip`; new: `to_test_case(c, chunk, category, *, corpus_version, split, difficulty, extra_chunk)`, `license_config_of`, `cross_check`, `validate_candidates`, `check_datagen_model`, `datagen_policy`, `build_datagen_client`. A cross-lingual pair is asked in either direction (a seeded coin): an English question is answered from the German chunk and a German question from the English chunk; `cluster_id` is the German document in both cases and `gold_doc_ids` holds the gold chunk's document. `to_test_case`: `cluster_id` = the source document (first chunk of a multi-hop pair), `base_id == id`, `source="generated"`, normalised `gold_citations` (the chunk's `citation_key` when the model gave none), `license_config` from the sources (law + decision = `oldp_derived`), `split` is provisional (`assign_splits` sets the real one).
