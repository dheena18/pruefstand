[T3.1b follow-up 1 of D1287] The D222 fallback is applied to the tier-2 configs: `rerank.on_retrieve: false` in `apps/rag-demo/configs/rag.gpu.yaml` and in all 14 of its one-switch copies, changed together (`configs/bugs/gpu/*.yaml` ×9, `configs/cause/*.yaml` ×5). So the one-switch tests still hold.

**What the switch does.** D271's flag acts per endpoint and per config, not per stratum. Every `/v1/retrieve` of these configs skips the reranker, and `/v1/query` keeps it. In PR runs `/v1/retrieve` serves:
- `robust_pr`;
- with the default 150-case judged subset (D655), the `core` answerable cases outside the subset. Their retrieval metrics become first-stage too. Baseline and candidate route them identically.

**Not affected:** nightly runs generate every case through `/v1/query`, so they keep the reranker.

**Lane.** The reranked PR lane drops from 51.8 to about 15.7 min with the subset; it would be 22.9 min on a full `core` run.

**Cost.** The change moves the rag-demo `config_hash`, and with it the tier-2 target fingerprint. So it must land before the first tier-2 baseline.

**RQ1 is open.** The RQ1 cells (`rag.gpu.yaml` plus overrides, `/v1/retrieve` only) now measure without the reranker. T7.3 decides before its first run: either an RQ1 override `rerank.on_retrieve: true`, or a recorded note that RQ1 measures the first stage.
