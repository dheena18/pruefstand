[T4.7a, perturb and attacks stages] `perturb`: every filtered candidate becomes a base `TestCase` (`to_test_case`); a pair that would mix two share-alike licence configs is dropped and counted as `dropped_mixed_license`. Bases are ranked by applicability (number of perturbations that change the question; deterministic ones by running them, LLM-assisted ones counted as applicable for German questions, W3.14); deterministic variants are made for every German base, LLM-assisted ones (rewrite pass, then validity pass of T4.5) only for the `perturb.llm_bases` top-ranked `shared_public` bases because the external rewriter never sees private-tenant text; a local `models.rewrite_local` model for private tenants is a follow-up. `attacks`: injection cases from `build_attack_set` (only with `corpus_config`; versions compared by `check_attack_versions`), leak and filter-bypass probes only for customer tenants that own a canary (a probe needs two such tenants), system-prompt probes always, the own refusal templates against the citation keys each tenant can retrieve (`check_refusal_laws: false` for a slice corpus that lacks most laws). All attack cases get the real `corpus_version`. |
