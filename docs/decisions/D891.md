[T4.4a, perturb polish] Limits of D394 closed; the six changed perturbations get `version = "2"`. The version is informational today: no cache key, hash or manifest uses the version of a deterministic perturbation, and `build_dataset --resume` reuses existing variants (follow-up: put `{name: version}` of the deterministic perturbations into the perturb stage settings and the manifest `prompt_versions`). `numbers`: `10.000,- €` → `10000 EUR`, scale words (`Mio.`, `Mrd.`, `Tsd.`, `Millionen`, `Milliarden`) count as a unit (`3,5 Mio. €` → `3.5 Mio. EUR`), no blank-type grouping at all: only `.` groups thousands, a number written with a plain blank, no-break space or U+202F (`1 000,50 €`, `§ 3 100 €`, `Tel. 030 123 456 €`) is ambiguous and left alone, and a number that follows `digit + blank` is never converted on its own (so `§ 3 100 €` and `Abs. 2 500 Euro` keep their reference number); tokens with `://` or `@` are never touched, `Euro` after a number or scale word becomes `EUR` like `€`, a euro sign glued to a letter or doubled (`€uro`, `€€`) leaves the text unchanged, `1234,567 €` stays unchanged (three decimals look like a German group). `dates`: no rewrite after `Az.`, `Aktenzeichen:`, `Nr.`, `Version`, `Release` (colon allowed). `umlaut_translit`: a capital umlaut next to a capital becomes `AE` / `OE` / `UE` (`ÄRGER` → `AERGER`). `abbreviations`: capitalised `Z. B.`, `Ggf.`, `Bzw.`, `I.V.m.` and `usw.` (→ `und so weiter`, the full stop stays at a sentence end; `USW.` → `UND SO WEITER`) are expanded (`Abs.` / `Nr.` only in their usual spelling). `typos`: every word of any law alias, also multi-word ones, is protected (`match_law` at the words that can open an alias), domains with non-ASCII endings (`kanzlei.köln`) are protected like ASCII ones, and a hex run after an all-caps token is the tail of a canary written with spaces. `citation_style`: an en dash, em dash or non-breaking hyphen glued to the law name makes a compound like `-` does. A property test checks that a URL survives byte for byte and the citations parse to the same list under typos, umlaut_translit, eszett, numbers, dates and abbreviations.
