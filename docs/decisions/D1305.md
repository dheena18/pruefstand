[T6.2b S4, nightly helpers] `gate_orchestrator/nightly.py` holds the pure scheduling rules of the nightly, as functions of an injected clock and plain data (no I/O; the `gate-orchestrator` subcommands read the state files and call them): `release_run_due` (any of `release-b0..b2` missing for the current fingerprint, D321), `cause_switches_due` (Berlin Sunday of the start, or forced; D223), `nightly_alarm` (D281: `nightly-failed` at once on a hard or absolute fail, on a statistical fail only when the previous completed night also failed), `nightly_judge` (the catalogue `judge` entry on `gpu1`; a configured judge missing from the catalogue gives `judge_fallback=True`; ADR-0019, D329), `prune_due` (first poll of a new UTC month, so a missed day 1 still prunes; D110) and the promotion selection (`promotable`, `promotable_nightly`: newest complete, unpromoted run without `judge_fallback`, `judge_drift`, `judge_degraded`, a judged metric informational for truncation or an `infra_error`; `promotion_refs`: `nightly-b<k>`, or `release-b0..b2` for a release run; D321). `night-guard`, `backup-check` and the CLI entry point are owned by T6.2a's orchestrator CLI; `window.py` keeps the deadline and phase rules.
