[T5.4d, drift anchors] Follow-up of D945. The stored verdict of a drift anchor is the **majority of 5 runs** (`judges.drift.majority_scores`, ties to the earliest run; `AnchorItem.majority_runs`, default 1 = T5.4 files), and the flip rate is counted on **4 further runs** against it (`count_flips`), so stored verdict and flip rate come from different runs (D934 rule otherwise unchanged: Wilson upper bound over comparisons, smallest c >= 5 with P(Bin(50, f_upper) >= c) <= 1 %, cap 10). `a0` is now the agreement of the first counted run with the stored majority, which supersedes D934's "run 2 vs run 1" for majority anchor files (still recorded only, D253). Every run's scores are kept in `anchor_flips.json`. Three 9-run passes of `gemma3:12b` (cache bypassed, 4 in flight): **pass 1** (`results/rq2/anchor_flips_screen.json`): 4 flips / 200, threshold 8; a04 (`faithfulness_constructed:f4824f68f7e37f53`) 2 in 3 of 9 runs, 1 in 6. **Pass 2** (a04 replaced, `anchor_flips_screen2.json`): 1 flip / 200, threshold 6; pooled over 18 runs a00 (`...27d0deb41e81bc41`) takes all three scores 0 / 1 / 2 and a02 (`...6df752e3f292018d`) jumps 2 <-> 0, each odd in about 1 of 9 runs. **Pass 3** (a00, a02, a04 replaced by the owner rule of D1251, pooled screens, a fresh independent run set; `anchor_flips.json`): 1 flip in 200 counted comparisons (rate 0.005, Wilson upper 0.028), `a0` 1.0, 1 changed anchor (the new a02, `...fa6a75f783971e30`, 1 vs 2 once in 9: adjacent, kept) -> `drift_threshold` **6** (P(Bin(50, 0.028) >= 5) > 1 %), not 5; `report.py` wrote `drift_threshold: 6`, `a0: 1.0`, `flip_rate_upper: 0.028`. No further screening (no fishing for 5). The comparison-level Wilson bound treats the repeats of one anchor as independent; an anchor-level bound (1 changing anchor of 50 -> Wilson upper 0.105) would make the judge unfit, but for false alarms the per-anchor binomial is conservative (well under one changed anchor expected per night), so threshold 6 is safe. The D945 caveat is now mitigated (majority verdicts, separate counted runs, the 3 non-adjacent or back-and-forth anchors replaced), not removed: an occasional adjacent single flip remains. Caveat: replacing unstable anchors makes the set more stable than the judge is on average (selection effect). The drift cassettes were re-recorded from real calls. `judge_epoch`: D1251.
