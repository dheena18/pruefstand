[T1.13a, rag-demo review minors] (a) The 413 limit is a pure ASGI middleware (`rag_demo/body_limit.py`) that counts the bytes in `receive`, so a chunked body without `Content-Length` (or with a lying one) is refused at 16 KiB; the application then sees a disconnect and its later output is dropped (FastAPI would turn the aborted read into a 400). (b) With `injection_guard: on` a chunk text is NFKC-normalised, stripped of zero-width characters and **every** `<` is escaped to `&lt;` (was: only `</` before `dokument`), so `</ dokument>`, `< /dokument>`, newline and zero-width splits, fullwidth brackets and a forged opening `<dokument id=.. quelle=..>` cannot appear in the prompt; texts with NFKC compatibility characters (superscripts, ligatures) reach the model in their normalised form. (c) `record_cassettes.sh` writes the model id and SHA-256 (generator) or revision and weights SHA-256 (TEI) into the rag-demo cassette headers (D353); `--rag-demo-headers` rewrites the committed headers in place keeping date and git sha (no re-recording); the Ollama and reranker cassettes keep their own stages and carry no header yet. (d) `GUARD_NOTE` and the English-switch sentence moved to `prompts/guard_note_v1.txt` and `prompts/answer_language_en_v1.txt` (text unchanged, `config_hash` unchanged). (e) `test_isolation.py` scans sources, configs, scripts, Dockerfiles and `*.ts(x)`/`*.js` of `apps/` outside tests, fixtures and build output, not only `*.py` (JSON content files are excluded: the dashboard licence texts name the datasets).
