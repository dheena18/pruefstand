[T6.2a S7, review of #170] (1) The infra status text must fit GitHub's 140-character description with its ending intact: `infra_summary` shortens the `<kind>: <detail>` part (ending with `…`) so that `"executor infra error: <summary>"` is at most 140 characters and still ends with `"; retrying once"` / `"; re-dispatch to retry"` (the part the owner acts on); `run.json` keeps the full `infra_error`. Long post-run details (exit 15 placement rows, exit 11 endpoint rows) hit this. (2) `subprocess_runner` decodes UTF-8 with replacement (not the locale) and merges stderr into the output, so a script-level `health.sh` exit (1, 2, 64) names its last output line (at most 60 characters) in the `executor` infra error. (3) The process-group tests patch the TERM grace and drain to 0.3 s and pin that the pipe is closed and the final reap is bounded.
