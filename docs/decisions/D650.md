section: "Task decisions (appended)"

[T3.1a, lead, 2026-11-03] `rerank-cpu` takes its torch thread count from the container's cgroup CPU quota (`cpu_limit()` in `rerank_app.py`: cgroup v2 `cpu.max` or v1 `cpu.cfs_*`, capped by the visible cores, `RERANK_THREADS` overrides), not from a hard-coded number; it sets `OMP_NUM_THREADS`/`MKL_NUM_THREADS` before torch loads and calls `torch.set_num_threads`. The VM has 160 cores and the container is capped to 8 CPUs by the CFS quota; torch started one thread per host core and was throttled: a 2-text rerank took 24 s, 0.18 s after the fix. Without this the tier-2 GPU tests timed out at the 60 s `request_timeout_s` (503 `backend unavailable`).
