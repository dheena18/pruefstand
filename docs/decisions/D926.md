[CI, curl retries] Every `curl` in a `run:` step of `.github/workflows/*.yml` carries `--retry 3 --retry-all-errors --retry-delay 5` (lychee, gitleaks, actionlint downloads failed several times on `curl: (22) ... 504`); the SHA-256 check after each download is unchanged, no permission and no `uses:` pin changes. The workflow guard (`scripts/workflow_curl.py`, called from `check_workflows.py`) fails a workflow whose curl lacks any of the three flags.
