[T3.3, compound splitter] An own dictionary splitter (`rag_demo/analysis/compound.py`) on a shipped lexicon (`lexicon_de.txt`, own list of ~330 German legal, HR and everyday word stems taken from the slice corpus, MIT with the repo, < 5 kB), not `charsplit`: the PyPI package `charsplit` 0.1.2 is an unrelated project that has no n-gram splitter, and the original CharSplit is not on PyPI. A word of 10 to 60 letters is split only if the lexicon covers it completely; linking elements `s es n en e er ens` between parts, the last part may carry an inflection ending (`n en e er ern s es ses`, so `Kündigungsfristen` gives `frist`); fewest parts, then fewest linking/ending characters; unknown words stay whole (false negatives preferred over false splits). Measured on the committed fixtures by a hand-labelled test (`test_compound.py`). Lexicon extension for the RQ1 compound axis is task **T3.3a** (`tasks/TASKS.md`)
