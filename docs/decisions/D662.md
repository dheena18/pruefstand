id: "D662"
section: "Task decisions (appended by tasks)"
---

[ops, owner decision 2026-11-03] `status.sh` (and so `health.sh`) report `disk-models` as WARN when the models disk has less than the reserve plus 1 GB free (reserve 2 GB, so below 3 GB; detail `free_kb=… min_free_gb=2 warn_below_gb=3`); the pull rule is unchanged (a pull needs model size plus the 2 GB reserve, exit 17 otherwise). The lead decides pulls and removals of catalogue models by itself under D451 (never the standard generator) and removes what the current tasks do not need to make room for the next pull; every one is logged in `gpu-cluster.md` section 8. The change is in `infra/cluster/status.sh`, so it takes effect after the owner's `make gpu-deploy-bin`. Supersedes the WARN-below-2-GB wording of D328 and ADR-0019 (`interfaces.md` and `gpu-cluster.md` are updated).
