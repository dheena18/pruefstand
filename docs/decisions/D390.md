[T4.4] `numbers` rewrites a German number only when the reading is unambiguous: thousands-grouped with a decimal part, with two or more groups, or with a unit (`%`, `€`, `EUR`, `Euro`, `Prozent`) next to it; a plain decimal comma only with such a unit; `Abs. 2,3`, `Nr. 1,2`, `§ 622`, integers < 1000 and dates stay. `€` always becomes `EUR` (`250€` → `250 EUR`, `€ 99,90` → `EUR 99.90`). If any German-format number would remain after the rewrite (`zwischen 1.000 und 5.000 €`, `Abs. 2,3 und 13,7 %`, `13,375 %`) the whole text stays unchanged (no half-converted sentence; review of PR #29)
