[T5.2b, refusal] `refusal_judged` is 1.0 when the answer refused, `details["refused_source"]` is `phrase` or `judge`. The phrase list decides; the judge is asked only for an ambiguous answer (no phrase and a hedge from `metrics/data/refusal_hedges.txt` v1, or at most 40 words without citation (`resp.citations` or `§`) and without a digit) and only when T5.4 marked the refusal judge passing (`judges/data/calibration_status.yaml`: `refusal_judged: {passed: true}`; no file = off, so until T5.4 the phrase list alone decides). The prompt never sees `expect_refusal`. `runner/refusal.py` `finalise_refusal(case, values, judged)` rewrites `refusal_correct` / `false_refusal` to 1.0 on a judge verdict "refused" and marks both with `refused_source`. `refusal_flag_mismatch` is counted once per case (the deterministic refusal metrics and `refusal_judged` can both carry it)
