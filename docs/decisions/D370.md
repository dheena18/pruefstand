[T2.4] `parallel_id` is `<celex>:<block id>`: `32016R0679:art-6` for an Article, `32016R0679:art-6:abs-2` for paragraph 2 of an Article split by paragraph, `32016R0679:rec-1-5` for a block of recitals (`rec-10` for a lone one); German chunk and English twin carry the same value. `ArticleChunker` splits an Article when its body has more than 1 500 tokens **and** the body starts with the marker of paragraph 1 (`1.` or `(1)`, numbers without gaps; an Article that opens with an unnumbered sentence is a list of definitions and is never split); `chunk_aligned` splits an Article in both languages when it is long in either, and raises `DatasetError` when the chunk ids still differ. T2.5 must call `chunk_aligned` for every (DE, EN) pair (not `ArticleChunker.chunk` per document) and treat its `DatasetError` as a misaligned act; the CLI does so and subtracts such an act from `acts`. `citation_key` stays the Article key (`Art. 6 DSGVO`) for all pieces, the piece is named by `section="Art. 6 Abs. 1"`.
