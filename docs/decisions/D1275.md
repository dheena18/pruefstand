[T6.3 offline core, before the noise runs] Definitions used by `experiments/noise/`: a run pair's **σ_d** is the standard deviation (ddof 1) of its **cluster-mean** paired differences (candidate - baseline; case diffs of one cluster averaged first, matching the gate's cluster resampling); σ_d of a no-op or churn kind, and of an arm (`kind == "all"`), is pooled over its pairs as the root mean of the variances; the MDE row uses `se = σ_d / sqrt(applicable clusters)` and `minimum_detectable_effect(se, alpha, δ, m = Holm family size)`, flags MDE > 1.5 δ with σ_d(churn) and then reports the smallest cluster count that meets 1.5 δ (δ is never changed); rows below `min_clusters` are marked not gated. The A/A replay shifts every metric with a σ_d by one N(0, σ_d) draw per cluster (shared by the cluster's cases, clipped to [0, 1], `latency_ms` untouched) and runs the production `evaluate_gate`; FAIL and WARN-or-FAIL (UNSUPPORTED_CONFIG counted as WARN, INSUFFICIENT_DATA as neither) carry Wilson 95 % intervals. `check_consistency.py` compares `pruefstand.yaml` with the SPEC §5.6 `gate:` block key by key (thresholds, slices, alpha, warn_p, min_clusters, resamples, correction, hard fails); an intended difference needs an entry naming its decision.
