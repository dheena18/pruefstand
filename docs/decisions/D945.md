[T5.4 review M3] A drift threshold is capped at twice the N3 default (`threshold_cap(n) = 2 * max(3, ceil(0.1 n))`, n = 50 -> 10): `drift_threshold` raises (`judge unfit`) when the measured flip-rate bound needs more, `is_drift` refuses a threshold above the cap, and `judges.status` refuses a stored `drift_threshold` above 10 (`ConfigError`); `flips.flip_summary` records `drift_threshold: null` for an unfit judge. `gemma3:12b` (9) is within the cap. Caveat: its 4 flips sit on only 2 of the 50 anchors (`changed_anchors: 2`), so the per-anchor binomial overstates the spread; the per-run verdicts of the 4 judgings were not stored, so a majority verdict cannot be stored without a GPU run. Replacing those 2 anchors or storing the majority of >= 5 runs (and re-deriving the threshold, likely 5) is a follow-up.
