[T4.5, gender] Rule table `data/gender_nouns.yaml` with 50 frequent generic masculines (singular, plural, feminine plural, star stem, optional participle). Plurals (nominative, accusative, dative) are rewritten in one seeded style per text: `star` Mitarbeiter*innen, `colon` Mitarbeiter:innen, `partizip` Mitarbeitende (only when every noun has a lexical participle), `pair` Mitarbeiterinnen und Mitarbeiter; same seed, same style. A singular or a form behind a singular determiner (`der Mitarbeiter`, `den Kunden`) needs article and verb agreement and goes to the model, as does every noun outside the table (a text without a table noun is `not_applicable`: no model call, since a rule cannot find unknown nouns).
