[T3.3a, compound lexicon for the RQ1 axis] `lexicon_de.txt` grows from 322 to 449 stems (3.9 kB, still < 5 kB of D460). Mined from every German word of >= 10 letters in `corpus/slice_v1`, `datasets/fixtures` and `datasets/calibration/v1` (8 245 distinct words; the committed corpus and fixtures hold 546 of them): compounds the splitter missed (e.g. `Geschäftsordnung`, `Rufbereitschaft`, `Schwerbehinderung`, `Mahngebühr`, `Dienststelle`, `personenbezogen`) were covered by adding domain stems (HR, data protection, rent, insurance, contracts); stems that would turn `Kündigungs-`, `Betriebs-` or `Widerrufs-` into a part of their own, whole-word compounds and the false-split makers (`schreibung`, `hof`, `team`, `mehr`) were left out. Split behaviour of the existing gold words is unchanged. Known false splits stay on main as before (false negatives are preferred, D460; they need a rule, not a stem): participles on `-ende` (`arbeitende`), `dauerhafte`, `mangelhafte`. `kleinbauern` was a new false split of the first draft and is fixed by the stem `bauer`. Whole-compound stems make some splits coarser than before (`Mutterschutzgesetz` = mutterschutz+gesetz, `Pflegezeitgesetz`, `Teilzeitbeschäftigung`); `krankenkasse` was dropped because it split correctly (krank+kasse) before. `test_compound.py` now checks the reviewed split set `compound_gold_de.txt` (every split of the committed corpus and fixture text; a regression lock, circular, NOT an independent recall figure), that every other long word of that text stays whole (a new split fails until reviewed) and 24 simple long words (precision), plus lexicon size and order. `compound_known_misses_de.txt` lists 20 compounds of that text that should split but do not (`recyclinghof`, `volkshochschule`, `betroffenenrechte` ...) as input for a real recall figure (proposed T3.3b). The analyzer digest of the index fingerprint changes (D461, D490): the next `rag-demo index` builds a new collection (`...__a7d93f0d__...`); no committed index exists, the contract examples were re-recorded on it (D913). Only `corpus/slice_v1` and the `datasets/` tree exist on the VM (the data root holds no raw corpora yet), so the mining covers those; mining the full downloaded corpora is a follow-up once `data/` has fetched them.
