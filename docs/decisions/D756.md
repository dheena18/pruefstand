[T4.7a, what the offline and live tests cover] `test_build_dataset_offline.py` runs all six stages on the real slice corpus without a model: the candidates are hand-written for real chunks (`generation.candidates_from`), the support check is off (reported as `support_skipped`) and the embeddings are REAL bge-m3 vectors of the 12 questions, recorded through Ollama's native `/api/embed` under `pruefstand-lock` (`tests/cassettes/dataset_embed/bge_m3_questions.json`, `record_cassettes.sh --review`, ADR-0008; replay raises on an unrecorded text); it covers stage files, resume, ranking, licence drops, splits and validation. The datagen, support and rewrite models run in `tests/e2e/test_build_dataset_live.py` (marks `e2e live network`; needs `PRUEFSTAND_GH_MODELS_TOKEN`, TEI and the `live` stack; `models.github.ai` is not reachable from the VM, D604), so that row of the test matrix stays `weak` until the owner or an Actions job runs it. The CPU-slice reviewer (a non-qwen GGUF through the `llm` image) is not set up; the review is covered by the A40 cassettes (D755). |
