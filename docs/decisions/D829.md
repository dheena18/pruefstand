[T4.3, review round 2 of #76] (1) Evidence scopes live in `dataset/filters_scope.py`: Absatz markers are language-aware (German `(n) `, English EUR-Lex `n. ` at the start of a line); the text before the first marker is the heading of a first chunk (never evidence, also when the citation names no Absatz) or, in a later chunk, the continuation of the Absatz in which the PREVIOUS chunk(s) of the same document end (found through the chunk mapping; unknown previous chunk = not accepted) and counts only for exactly that Absatz and only if it comes before this chunk's first marker; a chunk without markers is a lone Absatz 1 (first chunk) or a continuation (later chunk); an Absatz that exists nowhere and Absatz 0 are rejected; adjacent cited Absätze (`Abs. 1 und 2`, `1 bis 3`) form one text so evidence may span them; the evidence must match on word boundaries and Absatz markers do not count toward the 3-word minimum; in a citation of several norms (`;`, `und § ...`) a range applies to its own norm only. (2) The copy rule drops only the KNOWN law abbreviations (`_LAWS`) and `*gesetz`/`*verordnung` names, so an all-caps verbatim copy is still caught. (3) Support verdicts are keyed by `support_key` = sha256 of candidate id, question, answer, the texts of all chunks, the support prompt (name + text hash) and the model; the verdict log and `SupportSetup.known/on_verdict` use it, so a changed answer, chunk text, prompt or model is judged again; verdicts are matched to candidates by position, so two candidates with the same question but different answers are both judged. (4) `filter_report.json` has `complete`; a run deletes the other two kept-file names (`filtered.jsonl`, `filtered_partial.jsonl`, `filtered_nosupport.jsonl`).
