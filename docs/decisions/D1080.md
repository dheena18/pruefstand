[T6.2a S7, re-check of #170] The infra set of `run_with_retry` is only `InfraError` and `subprocess.SubprocessError`: a plain `OSError` (e.g. a candidate artefact's `FileNotFoundError`/`PermissionError`) is never retried or labelled infra; callers wrap the OSErrors they know are infra (Ollama connection refused, tunnel down, data root gone) in `InfraError` with its kind. A `SubprocessError` gets kind `executor` (`executor: health.sh --repair failed (<Type>)` / `executor: <Type> during the run`), never `ollama_down`. A non-infra exception from the second attempt (after "retrying once" was posted) posts `post_status` `error` "executor error: <Type>; re-dispatch to retry", records `result: "error"` and re-raises, so a PR is never left at "retrying once"; from the first attempt nothing was posted and it just propagates
