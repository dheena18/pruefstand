[T6.2a, heartbeat during a dispatch] `run_gate_job` refreshes the heartbeat from the moment it starts waiting for the GPU lock until the job ends: a daemon thread (`ticker.ticking`) pushes `last_job` at once and every 15 minutes (Y5.1, D225), the payload gains the four health fields as `"true"` with empty `*_false_since` once the preflight under the lock has passed (before that nothing is claimed about health), and the job's end pushes `last_result` once more. Pushes merge into the stored payload (`heartbeat_store.update`, string values only) and use `DASHBOARD_DATA_PAT`, which `gate-executor.yml` already passes; a failed push is logged and retried at the next tick, never fatal. Follow-up, not built: committing `state/processed.jsonl` to the private branch `executor-state` (T6.2a "state sync"); until then it lives only on the VM disk under `$PRUEFSTAND_HOME/state/`.
