[T3.1e, fail closed, what the scan covers] The redacted text is held back and scanned once more; the scan is a safety net, not a second redaction. It looks for the login, the host name, `$HOME`, the models dir, `/data/` and `/home/` paths, any IPv4 other than `127.0.0.1:11434`, IPv6 with `::`, URL hosts, `user@host`, GPU uuids, sha256 digests, 32+ character alphanumeric runs and secret-looking pairs. It does NOT cover bare hostnames, dotted `host:port` or plain `port N` values and path-like long identifiers: those depend on the sed rules alone. A failed tail, a failed sed or a scan hit prints nothing on stdout and exits non-zero (1 log or cluster.env unusable, 5 redaction failed or leak, 64 usage); the message names no log content. `log-tail.sh` is in `SHA256SUMS` like every `infra/cluster/*.sh` (`bin-integrity`, `bin-sums`, health.sh exit 14), so the owner redeploys with `make gpu-deploy-bin` after the merge
