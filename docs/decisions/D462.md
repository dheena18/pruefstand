[T3.3, number normalisation] German thousands dots are removed and the decimal comma becomes `.` (`1.000,50` -> `1000.50`, `3,5` -> `3.5`; dates `01.02.2024` and lists `1,2,3` are left alone), `€` becomes the token `eur`; numbers are never stemmed
