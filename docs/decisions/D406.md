[T1.10] The dataset loader treats the files as untrusted: regular files only (no symlink), lines over 1 MB, files over 512 MiB and datasets over 500 000 cases are refused, errors name `file:line` and never quote the row, a row's `split` and `corpus_version` must agree with its file and `dataset_manifest.json`, case ids are unique across the loaded splits, and a split count must equal the manifest.
