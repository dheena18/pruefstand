[T4.3, evidence, citation, cluster, style] (1) `check_evidence`: a citation that parses but names none of the source paragraphs is `citation_mismatch`; an `evidence_span` must be a span of the cited Absatz of a chunk THE CITATION NAMES (`evidence_off_paragraph`), so the whole chunk is accepted only for a citation without Absatz or a chunk without Absätze (T4.2's `check_candidate` accepted the span in any source chunk). A missing citation or evidence is no reason. (2) `drop_cluster_overlap`: the cluster of a case is its source document (the German one of a cross-lingual pair, D785); a multi-hop pair whose second document is the cluster of another case (or of an earlier kept pair) is `cluster_overlap` and dropped, single-chunk candidates never; decided greedily in stable-id order, so the result is input-order independent; the document group is `parallel_id` or `doc_id`. (3) `style_report`: laie / (laie + fachlich) of the KEPT set against `min_laie_share` (0.6), evaluated from `style_min_n` (20) styled candidates on; it is report-level (`filter_report.json`, a warning line), nothing is dropped for it.
