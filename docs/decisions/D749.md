[T4.7a, review sampling and apply] `sample_for_review(cases, seed, full_fraction=0.2, min_per_category=30)`: every `smoke` and `core` case and every attack or probe case (any split) is reviewed; of `full`, `robust_pr` and `rq1` a seeded 20 % per stratum (`<category>/<generation prompt version>`, rounded up), topped up to 30 per category where the category has that many. `apply_decisions`: a `reject` removes the case and its whole cluster (`cluster_id`) from every split; a `fix` replaces the question, sets `source="generated+model-reviewed"` and drops the perturbed variants of that base (their text derives from the old question); one `ReviewRound` per generation prompt version with `reviewer="model:<id>@review_item_de_v1"`, numbering continues after the manifest's rounds; `error_rate = (fix + reject) / reviewed` per stratum, above 10 % (strictly) the stratum `needs_regeneration`. Items without a model call (attack and probe cases) carry `model="deterministic"` and no rate. |
