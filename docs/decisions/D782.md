[T4.2, `evidence_span`] `QACandidate.evidence_span` is the model's `evidence` after whitespace and soft-hyphen normalisation when it is a substring of the cited chunk, lies inside the cited Absatz and has at most 60 tokens, else None with `counts["evidence_invalid"]` (never repaired). A token is a word or one punctuation mark (`\w+|[^\w\s]`), a conservative stand-in for the model tokenizer. The Absatz is cut at the markers `(n)` of the normalised chunk text; a chunk without markers accepts any cited Absatz; a cited Absatz that is not in the chunk is accepted only for text before the first marker (a continuation chunk). For a pair the span is checked against both chunks (multi-hop) or the gold chunk (cross-lingual).
