[T4.4] `typos` makes one edit in one word of >= 6 letters: deletion, adjacent transposition of two different letters, or substitution by a neighbouring QWERTZ key (case kept); the first letter is never edited; letter runs over 40 characters are not words (bounded work per edit); words that are a single-word law name or alias (full multi-word names are not protected) or have a capital after the first letter (`KSchG`, `DSGVO`) are skipped; edit distance 1 is Damerau-Levenshtein (a transposition counts 1). The edit type is drawn uniformly among the available types, then uniformly among its variants, all from the passed `Generator`
