## Quick power estimate (basis for S1–S3)

The FAIL rule is: Holm-adjusted p < α=0.025, which means the upper bound of the one-sided CI must be below −δ.
Normal approximation: SE = σ_d/√n, P(FAIL | true drop Δ) = Φ((Δ−δ)/SE − z), with z = 1.96 (one test) or
z = 2.95 for Holm across m=16 slice tests (the overall slice has the smallest p, so it is compared against α/m).
Here σ_d is the SD of the per-case paired difference. For judged 1–5 scores and binary metrics, 0.25–0.40 is
realistic once the target cache is invalidated (any image change) and vLLM's nondeterminism comes in.

| n clusters | σ_d | δ | P(PASS \| no change), one slice | P(FAIL) at Δ=2δ, m=1 / m=16 | P(FAIL) at Δ=3δ, m=1 / m=16 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 300 | 0.15 | 0.03 | 0.93 | 0.93 / 0.69 | 1.00 / 1.00 |
| 300 | 0.25 | 0.03 | 0.55 | 0.55 / 0.19 | 0.99 / 0.89 |
| 300 | 0.40 | 0.03 | 0.25 | 0.25 / 0.05 | 0.74 / 0.36 |
| 300 | 0.25 | 0.05 | 0.93 | 0.93 / 0.69 | 1.00 / 1.00 |
| 50 (smoke) | 0.25 | 0.03 | 0.13 | 0.13 / 0.02 | 0.40 / 0.10 |
| 33 (one category slice) | 0.25 | 0.05 | 0.21 | 0.21 / 0.04 | 0.63 / 0.26 |

At Δ = δ, P(FAIL) ≈ α by construction. **The gate is designed so that it almost never fails a real drop of
exactly `max_drop`.** For 80 % power at Δ=2δ with σ_d=0.25 and δ=0.03, you need n ≈ (2.8·0.25/0.03)² ≈ 545
clusters without correction, and ≈ 1,000 with Holm m=16.
