## Assumptions (planning numbers, not measured here; all **verify** in T0.6 / T3.1b)

- llama.cpp-class engines (Ollama) on an A40 (GA102, 696 GB/s): 27B `Q8_0` prefill ≈ 1 000–1 400 tok/s,
  decode ≈ 16–19 tok/s per stream, ≈ 45–60 tok/s aggregate at 4 slots; 24B `Q4_K_M` prefill ≈ 1 300–1 800,
  decode ≈ 80–110 tok/s aggregate; Gemma 3 27B `Q4_K_M` prefill ≈ 1 000–1 500, decode ≈ 70–90 aggregate;
  70B `Q4_K_M` layer-split over two cards (no tensor parallelism) prefill ≈ 400–600, decode ≈ 30–40 aggregate.
  Prefill does not scale with `OLLAMA_NUM_PARALLEL` (compute-bound); decode does, sub-linearly.
- `bge-reranker-v2-m3` (XLM-R-large, ≈ 300 M non-embedding params) on 8 vCPU fp32: ≈ 0.3–1 s per
  (query, 300–512-token passage) pair (≈ 160–330 GFLOP per pair). A § chunk is often 300–512 tokens.
- Ollama behaviour (**verify** against the pinned version; it changes between releases): the OpenAI-compatible
  `/v1/chat/completions` does not accept `options.num_ctx`; a request whose `options` (incl. `num_ctx`) differ
  from the loaded runner's triggers an unload + reload; a model the scheduler estimates too large is loaded
  with partial CPU offload without an error (`/api/ps` `size_vram < size`); `OLLAMA_CONTEXT_LENGTH` sets the
  server default context; a server start prunes unreferenced blobs unless `OLLAMA_NOPRUNE` is set.
- NAS read throughput unknown (T0.6 C6): at 1 GbE (~110 MB/s) the 29 GB generator loads in ≈ 4.5 min, a
  17 GB judge ≈ 2.5 min, the 43 GB 70B ≈ 6.5 min; at 10 GbE roughly 5–8× faster.
- Gemma-3-27B: 62 layers, 16 KV heads × 128 → ≈ 0.5 MiB/token f16 without sliding-window cache; 5:1
  local:global layers with a 1 024-token window → ≈ 90–110 KiB/token effective **if** Ollama sizes the SWA cache.
- Holm first step at m ≈ 40: MDE = max(4.07·SE, δ + 0.84·SE), SE = σ_d/√n_clusters (as in SPEC §7.2).
