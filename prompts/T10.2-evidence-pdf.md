## Goal

`pruefstand report --format evidence-pdf` renders the existing `EvidenceRecord` to a 2–4 page PDF a
release manager can attach to customer documentation.

## Context

Render HTML from a Jinja2 template, then PDF with WeasyPrint (new dependency as optional extra `pdf` of `packages/pruefstand/pyproject.toml`, recorded in `docs/decisions.md`; system libs in the Action
image; document the apt packages). Sections: release identity (versions, SHAs, digests), dataset and
corpus versions, models and judges (incl. `judge_mode` `github|local|skipped`, the judge serving version and
`judge_epoch` from the `eval_fingerprint` inputs (round 9 X3.3), the notices for `judge_fallback`, `judge_degraded`,
`judge_drift`, and the judge-truncated case count (X3.9)), thresholds, results with CIs and MDE, every informational
pair with its `informational_reason` (round 8 W3.6/W3.9/W3.3), `no_baseline` when set (`baseline_run_id` is then `None`, round 9 X5.5; the record says
"no comparison was made"), security results (leaks, injection rate, system-prompt leak), reviewer sign-off
field, artefact hashes, and a fixed disclaimer:
"Dieses Dokument unterstützt die Dokumentation zu Genauigkeit, Robustheit und Aufzeichnungspflichten
(vgl. EU AI Act); es stellt keine rechtliche Konformitätsbewertung dar." plus English version.
Optional mapping table: record section → AI Act theme (accuracy/robustness Art. 15, record-keeping
Art. 12) labelled "orientation only". Output deterministic (fixed fonts, no timestamps beyond the record's).
