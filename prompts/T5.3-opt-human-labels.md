## Goal

100 blind human labels on **real Prüfstand answers** (tier-2 runs of the reference app and of a weaker
catalogue generator), 25 per judged metric, so the report can show whether judge agreement measured
on the constructed sets (T5.4) also holds on the project's own answers, including partial credit.

## Context

- Pairs come from real run artefacts (`runs/<good>` and `runs/<weak>` on the `core` split); ≥ 40 % from
  the weak config so wrong answers occur. Labelers see question, reference answer, retrieved chunks and
  answer, never a judge score.
- Scales: faithfulness and answer_correctness 0/1/2; perturbation_consistency 0/1; refusal 0/1 meaning
  "is the answer a refusal?" (round 8 W3.5; labelers are not told whether a refusal was expected). The 25
  refusal pairs are drawn from **ambiguous** answers (T5.2b rule), the only ones the refusal judge sees.
- Each pair also carries `hard_case: bool` (the answer differs from the reference only in a §/number/date,
  a negation or a missing part, decided by the T5.3 construction helpers on the real text) so
  `compare_human.py` can report agreement on the hard subset next to T5.4's hard-negative specificity (X1.1; κ is never computed on the single-label hard subset).
- Annotator id is a pseudonym from env `PRUEFSTAND_ANNOTATOR`. No personal data.
- The tool lives in `tools/labeling_app/` (already in `testpaths` via `tools/*/tests`).
