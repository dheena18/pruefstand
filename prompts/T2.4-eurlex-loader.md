## Goal

`EurLexLoader` loads the 20 pinned acts in DE and EN; `ArticleChunker` produces one chunk per
Article (and per recital block), and every DE chunk and its EN twin share the same `parallel_id`
(e.g. `32016R0679:art-6`), so cross-lingual tests can check "same question, same facts".

## Context

- Input: `data/raw/eurlex/<celex>/{de,en}.html` (official HTML rendition, fixed in T2.1); parse with
  `lxml.html` (articles are `<p class="ti-art">` / `oj-ti-art` headings — detect both, test both).
- Citation keys: DE `"Art. 6 DSGVO"`, EN chunks also store the **canonical German key** so citation
  metrics match across languages. Canonical abbreviations come **only** from
  `core/data/law_aliases.yaml` (single source; e.g. GDPR→DSGVO, AI Act→KI-VO, NIS2→NIS2-RL, DSA→DSA);
  keep the English act label on the **document**: `Document.metadata["citation_en"]` = `"GDPR"` (round 9 D9-25: `Chunk` has no `metadata` field, interfaces §11, `extra="forbid"`); the English form of an article citation is `f"Article {n} {citation_en}"`, e.g. `"Article 6 GDPR"`, derived where needed, never stored per chunk.
- Recitals: chunk groups of 5 recitals (`section="Erwägungsgründe 1–5"` / `"Recitals 1–5"`).
- Articles longer than 1 500 tokens split by paragraph (`section="Art. 6 Abs. 1"`), still aligned.
- `license="EU-2011/833"`, `metadata["attribution"]` = "© European Union, https://eur-lex.europa.eu".
- Alignment check: DE and EN must have the same set of article numbers; mismatch → `DatasetError`
  for that act (log, skip act, count).
