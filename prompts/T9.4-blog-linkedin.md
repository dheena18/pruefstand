## Goal

An English blog post (~1,200 words, optional German version) and a LinkedIn post (≤ 1,300 characters)

## Context

Audience: ML/platform engineers and hiring managers in Germany. Angle: "RAG changes ship without proof —
here is a gate that blocks only real regressions and every tenant leak." Include: the problem, one
German failure-mode example (compound split), the blocked-PR GIF, the RQ3 table, judge cost finding
(RQ2), how to try it (quickstart), links (repo, PyPI, HF, demo, report). No legal-compliance claims.
Publishing is a human action; the AI never posts.
Claims must match the round-8 design (binding): the RQ3 headline number is the detection rate on the
**`statistical` stratum** (`{{rq3_detection_statistical}}`), with leaks/hard fails mentioned separately (every
gate catches those by construction); say that per-PR CPU checks (tier 1) decide on hard fails and the error rate
only (language, citation, refusal and injection checks are reported there; round 9 X5.6, X3.11) and that the
statistical gate runs on the GPU tier; the judge-cost finding is quoted with the round-9 pass rule (κ on the full
set plus hard-negative specificity with its Wilson lower bound, incl. the in-domain `legal_hard` set; X1.1, X3.1),
never a "hard-negative κ", and, if no judge passed calibration, as "judged metrics are informational" — never as a
calibrated result. The headline placeholder is the PR-tier `statistical`
detection rate on M1–M19 (X3.10). Operational claims follow round 11 (D222–D226): infrastructure failures are
reported as `error`, never blamed on the PR; the nightly covers `full` over 3 nights (refusal stratum every night);
no claim of per-night full coverage or of cross-judge comparisons.
